Scope and Method

This directory gathers churches, chapels, and monastic institutions cited across the database as architectural frameworks for medieval artistic production. The selection privileges sites that preserve, commissioned, and historically framed fresco cycles, panel painting programs, sculptural ensembles, and manuscript cultures.

Entries are listed in a research-oriented format and linked directly to their individual records for historical, iconographic, and bibliographic consultation.
